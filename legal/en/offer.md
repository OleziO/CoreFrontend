## 1. Parties

1.1. **Provider** — {{FOP_NAME}}, individual entrepreneur (FOP), RNOKPP (taxpayer number) {{TAX_ID}}, operating under the name Core League. Details are in section 15.

1.2. **User** — an individual who has accepted this Offer in the manner set out in section 4.

## 2. Definitions

2.1. **Platform** — the site {{DOMAIN}} and its services.

2.2. **Account** — the User's profile on the Platform, created through Discord login.

2.3. **Tournament** — a Dota 2 esports competition the Provider runs on the Platform. The terms of a specific Tournament (dates, format, requirements, fee, prize pool) are published in the **Tournament announcement** on the Platform.

2.4. **Entry fee** — a one-time payment by a team captain for the team's participation in a Tournament.

2.5. **VIP status** — a paid service giving access to extra Platform features for 1 month, renewing automatically every month.

2.6. **Voluntary support** — a one-time voluntary payment to support Core League, for which nothing is provided.

2.7. **Payment service** — WayForPay, through which card, Apple Pay and Google Pay payments are accepted.

2.8. **Regular payment** — the automatic monthly charge for VIP status to the User's card through the Payment service.

## 3. Subject of the agreement

3.1. The Provider supplies the following services to the User for a fee:

- organising the participation of the User's team in a Tournament (Entry fee);
- access to VIP status on the Platform.

3.2. The User may also make a Voluntary support payment. Such a payment is voluntary and does not oblige the Provider to provide anything in return.

3.3. The free features of the Platform are governed by the [Terms of Use](/terms/).

## 4. Acceptance of the Offer

4.1. The User accepts this Offer when they **both**:

- tick the box agreeing to this Offer, the [Terms of Use](/terms/) and the [Refund Policy](/refund/) before payment; and
- pay for the service concerned.

4.2. Once the payment succeeds, the agreement is concluded in the version the User accepted (see section 13).

4.3. By accepting the Offer, the User confirms they are at least 18 years old and entitled to use the payment card.

## 5. Prices

5.1. The prices of all services are shown on the [Prices](/prices/) page and on the payment page before the pay button is pressed. Prices are in Ukrainian hryvnia.

5.2. The Entry fee is set separately for each Tournament and published before registration opens.

5.3. Discounts (for example, for captains with active VIP status), where they apply, are shown on the [Prices](/prices/) page and included in the amount on the payment page.

5.4. The Provider may change prices. A new price does not apply to services already paid for. The Provider notifies the User of a change in the VIP price before the next charge. [LAWYER TO VERIFY: the notice period for a subscription price change]

## 6. Payment

6.1. Payment is made through the WayForPay Payment service by Visa or Mastercard card, and by Apple Pay or Google Pay if these methods are shown on the payment page.

6.2. Some payments accepted before the switch to WayForPay were processed through monobank internet acquiring.

6.3. A payment counts as made when the Payment service has confirmed it to the Provider.

6.4. If the card is in another currency, the User's bank may convert the amount at its own rate. The Provider is not responsible for exchange differences or fees of the User's bank.

6.5. An unpaid payment page is valid for a limited time and then expires (see the [Refund Policy](/refund/)).

## 7. Provision of services

7.1. **Entry fee.** After the payment is confirmed, the team is registered for the Tournament automatically. The service counts as provided once the team has taken part in its first Tournament match.

7.2. **VIP status.** Activated immediately after the payment is confirmed, for 1 month, and extended for the next month after each successful Regular payment.

7.3. **Voluntary support.** Nothing is provided.

7.4. A service is usually activated within 5 minutes of payment, at most within {{DELIVERY_MAX_HOURS}} hours. If this does not happen, the User contacts support as described in the [Terms of Use](/terms/).

## 8. Automatic renewal of VIP status

8.1. Before the first payment, the page shows the monthly charge amount and that the subscription renews automatically every month until cancelled.

8.2. At the first payment, the Payment service stores the card as a token for the Regular payment. The Provider has no access to the full card number.

8.3. Every month, on the day the paid period ends, the Payment service charges the same card the amount shown to the User before payment (or a new price notified to the User under clause 5.4).

8.4. If the charge fails, VIP status is not extended after the paid period ends.

8.5. The User may **cancel auto-renewal at any time** in the VIP window on the Platform: the VIP button at the top of the site → «Скасувати автопродовження» ("Cancel auto-renewal") → confirm. After cancellation there are no further charges, and VIP status stays active until the end of the period already paid for.

8.6. Deleting the Account also stops the Regular payment.
