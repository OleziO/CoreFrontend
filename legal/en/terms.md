## 3. Paid services

Current prices are on the [Prices](/prices/) page and on the payment page before you press "Pay". All amounts are in Ukrainian hryvnia.

### 3.1. Tournament entry fee

- A one-time payment **per team**, not per player.
- It is paid by the team **captain**. The site may show the amount per player — for reference only; players settle among themselves.
- The Organiser sets the fee separately for each tournament and shows it before payment.
- A captain with active VIP status gets a discount on the fee, stated on the [Prices](/prices/) page.
- A team's registration is confirmed only after payment.

### 3.2. VIP status (monthly subscription)

- VIP status unlocks extra features on the platform; they are listed in the VIP window on the site.
- The subscription is for 1 month and **renews automatically every month**: the amount is charged to the same card through a WayForPay regular payment until you cancel auto-renewal.
- You can cancel auto-renewal at any time in the VIP window (see the [Refund Policy](/refund/)). Access remains until the end of the period already paid for.

### 3.3. Voluntary support of Core League

- A one-time voluntary payment to support the league. Anyone can make it, including without logging in.
- The payer chooses the amount within the limits shown on the [Prices](/prices/) page.
- **Nothing is provided** in return: no goods, no services, no advantage in tournaments. It does not increase any prize pool.

## 4. Player duties

By using the platform you agree to:

- have **only one account** and play only from your own Steam account;
- give truthful profile data (country, positions, rating, MMR proof);
- play fair: no cheats, no third-party software that gives an advantage, no exploiting game bugs, no match fixing;
- not play from someone else's account, not hand your account to others and not play on an account with a deliberately lowered rating (smurfing);
- respect other players, admins and viewers: no insults, threats, discrimination, spam or deliberate disruption of matches;
- follow the [Tournament Rules](/rules/) and the admins' instructions during a tournament.

For breaches we may warn a player, restrict chat or features, disqualify a team or block an account. The consequences for payments are set out in the [Refund Policy](/refund/).

## 5. No reselling

Selling, buying or transferring for money tournament places, teams, team slots, or Core League, Steam or Discord accounts is prohibited. Detected cases lead to disqualification and blocking.

- A tournament's prize pool is **set by the Organiser and/or sponsors** and **announced before registration opens**.

- How the prize pool is split among prize places is stated in the tournament announcement.
- **How prizes are paid.** After the results are confirmed, the Organiser sends the prize money by bank transfer to the representative of the winning team (the captain or another person the team has named in writing). The payout period is stated in the tournament announcement.
