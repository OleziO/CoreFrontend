This Policy forms part of the [Public Offer Agreement](/offer/). Below are the rules for each service, the cases in which a refund is not possible, and how to apply.

## 1. Tournament entry fee

**We refund the full amount if:**

- the Organiser **cancels** the tournament;
- the Organiser **postpones** the tournament to another date and the team cannot or does not want to play on the new date;
- the team **withdraws at least {{WITHDRAW_DEADLINE_HOURS}} hours before the tournament starts** (the start time is stated in the tournament announcement);
- the money was **charged twice** for the same registration;
- the payment went through but, because of a **technical fault**, the team was not registered and the fault was not fixed before the tournament started.

**We do not refund if:**

- the team withdrew later than {{WITHDRAW_DEADLINE_HOURS}} hours before the tournament start;
- the tournament has already started or the team has played at least one tournament match;
- the team was disqualified for breaching the [Tournament Rules](/rules/) or the [Terms of Use](/terms/);
- the team did not show up for check-in or for a match.

The refund request is made by the **captain** who paid the fee. The money goes back to the card used for payment.

## 2. VIP status

- The current paid month is **not refunded**, including when you cancel auto-renewal in the middle of the period. After cancellation VIP stays active until the end of the paid period, and there are no further charges.
- **We refund** if:
  - a technical error occurred (for example, a double charge for the same period, or VIP not activated after payment and we could not fix it);
  - money was charged **after** you cancelled auto-renewal or deleted your account.

### How to cancel VIP auto-renewal

1. Press the **VIP** button at the top of the site.
2. In the VIP window press **«Скасувати автопродовження»** ("Cancel auto-renewal").
3. Confirm the cancellation.

After that there are no further charges. You can resume auto-renewal in the same window until the end of the paid period.

## 3. Voluntary support

A voluntary support payment is voluntary and nothing is provided for it, so as a rule it is not refunded. **We refund** only if:

- the money was charged twice;
- a technical error occurred during payment;
- you entered the wrong amount by accident.

The request must be sent **within {{SUPPORT_REFUND_WINDOW_DAYS}} days** of the payment date.

## 4. When a refund is not possible

No refund is made:

- for an entry fee — after the withdrawal deadline ({{WITHDRAW_DEADLINE_HOURS}} hours before the tournament start), after the tournament has started, after the team has played at least one match, after disqualification or a no-show;
- for VIP — for the current paid period, except for a technical error or a charge after cancellation;
- for voluntary support — after {{SUPPORT_REFUND_WINDOW_DAYS}} days or for reasons not listed in section 3;
- to a card, account or person other than the one the payment came from;
- in cash.

Prize money is not a User payment and is not returned through this procedure.

## 5. How to request a refund

Write to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} or {{SUPPORT_CHAT}} and give:

- your Discord username;
- what was paid for (entry fee, VIP or support) and the tournament name if it was an entry fee;
- the date and amount of the payment;
- the payment reference (it starts with `CORE-`, `VIP-` or `DON-`);
- the reason for the request.

**Timing:**

1. We review the request within {{SUPPORT_REVIEW_DAYS}} business days and tell you the decision.
2. If the request is approved, we start the refund through WayForPay within {{SUPPORT_REVIEW_DAYS}} business days of the decision. [LAWYER TO VERIFY: whether consumer protection law requires a shorter period]
3. The money goes back **to the same card** used for payment. This is how WayForPay works: "Сума клієнту буде повернута на ту ж карту з якої відбувався платіж" — the amount is returned to the card the payment was made from ([WayForPay, "Повернення коштів"](https://help.wayforpay.com/view/3342455), checked 2026-10-14).
4. How long the refund takes to appear on your account is decided by the bank that issued your card. WayForPay does not publish a specific period and refers to its "standard procedure" ([WayForPay, "Як повернути квитки?"](https://help.wayforpay.com/view/258756605), checked 2026-10-14). According to Visa Acceptance Solutions, refunds generally appear in the customer's account within 2 to 30 days after the merchant issues the refund, and the exact posting date is set by the card-issuing bank ([Visa Acceptance Solutions, "Payments - Refund Processing Timeframes"](https://support.visaacceptance.com/knowledgebase/knowledgearticle/?code=000001680), checked 2026-10-14). If the money has not arrived within 30 days of our refund notice, write to us and we will provide confirmation of the transaction for your bank. ## 6. What happens to the service after a refund

- **Entry fee:** the team is removed from the tournament if it has not started yet.
- **VIP:** VIP time is reduced by the period refunded; if the refund is linked to a cancellation, the regular payment is stopped.
- **Voluntary support:** the payment is marked as refunded.

## 7. Cancelling a payment before it is completed

If you opened a payment page but did not complete the payment, you do not need to do anything: the unpaid payment page simply expires and no money is charged.

- An **entry fee** payment page is valid for at most 24 hours, and in any case no longer than registration for the tournament stays open.
- A **VIP** payment page is valid for 1 hour.
- A **voluntary support** payment page is valid for 24 hours.

If your bank blocked (reserved) the amount but the payment was not completed, your bank releases the hold under its own rules. [CHECK WITH WAYFORPAY: how long holds on incomplete payments take to release]
