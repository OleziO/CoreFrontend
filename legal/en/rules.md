These rules apply to all Core League team tournaments. The details of a specific tournament are published in its **announcement** on the site. Items marked "**[per announcement]**" are set by the tournament announcement; if the announcement and these rules differ, the announcement prevails.

## 1. Format

- A team has **5 main players**, one of whom is the **captain**. Whether substitutes are allowed, and how many — **[per announcement]**.
- The tournament format (groups, playoff bracket, games per series), schedule and number of teams — **[per announcement]**.
- The captain represents the team: registers it, pays the entry fee, deals with the admins and receives the prize money unless the team has named another representative.

## 2. Who can take part

- Every player has logged in to the site with **Discord** and linked a **verified Steam account**.
- **One player — one account.** A player plays only from their own Steam account linked to their profile.
- A player may be on only one team within one tournament.
- Rating (MMR) requirements, team verification and MMR proof — **[per announcement]**. The admins may ask for a screenshot as MMR proof.
- Age requirements — as set out in the [Terms of Use](/terms/).

## 3. Registration and entry fee

- Registration opens and closes on the dates stated **[per announcement]**.
- If the tournament is paid, the entry fee is paid by the **captain** for the whole team. The fee is stated in the announcement and on the [Prices](/prices/) page.
- **Registration is confirmed only after payment.** An unpaid application does not hold a place in the tournament.
- Withdrawing a team and refunding the fee — under the [Refund Policy](/refund/).
- The prize pool is fixed, announced before registration opens and does not depend on the number of teams or the fees paid.

## 4. Schedule and check-in

- Match times and the check-in procedure (the team confirming it is ready before a match) — **[per announcement]**.
- A team that does not check in on time may be removed from the tournament without a refund of the fee.

## 5. Matches

- The lobby is created by a Core League bot or admin. Lobby settings (game mode, server, etc.) — **[per announcement]**.
- Only the team's registered players play, from their linked Steam accounts.
- **Pauses.** The pause limit — **[per announcement]**. A pause is allowed for a technical reason; the player who pauses briefly explains the reason in the game chat.
- **Technical issues.** If a game breaks for reasons outside the teams' control (Dota 2 server failure, mass disconnect), an admin decides whether to continue, restart or replay the game.
- **Technical loss.** A team that does not show up for a match or cannot field its roster within the waiting time stated **[per announcement]** receives a technical loss.

## 6. Results

- A match result is confirmed by the **Dota 2 match ID** (Steam and OpenDota data) or by an admin decision.
- Once confirmed, the result appears in the tournament bracket on the site.

## 7. Disputes

- The captain files a match dispute **within 24 hours** after the match ends, at {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} or {{SUPPORT_CHAT}}.
- Include the match ID, a description of what happened and evidence (screenshots, video).
- The admins' decision on a dispute is final within the tournament.

## 8. Disqualification

A team or player may be disqualified for:

- using cheats, third-party software or game bugs to gain an advantage;
- playing from someone else's account, handing over an account, or a player playing from an account other than the registered one;
- smurfing — playing on an account whose rating deliberately does not match the player's level;
- match fixing and deliberately losing;
- insults, threats, discrimination, persistent toxic behaviour;
- giving false information (including about MMR);
- reselling a tournament place;
- repeatedly ignoring admin instructions.

A disqualified team's entry fee is not refunded. A disqualified team loses its right to a prize place and prize money.

## 9. Prizes

- How the prize pool is split among prize places — **[per announcement]**.
- After the final results are confirmed, the Organiser sends the prize money by bank transfer to the **team representative** (the captain or another person the team has named in writing). The payout period — **[per announcement]**.
- Splitting prize money within the team is up to the team.
- Before the payout, the representative confirms their identity and ownership of the Steam and Discord accounts and provides details of a bank account in their own name. Without this the payout may be delayed.

## 10. Changes to the rules

The admins may clarify these rules. Changes affecting a tournament already open are published in the tournament announcement and in the official Core League Discord server and Telegram channel before the tournament starts.
