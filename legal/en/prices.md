This page lists everything you can pay for on Core League. All amounts are **in Ukrainian hryvnia (UAH)**. The table below is loaded directly from the system that supplies the amount on the payment page, so the prices here and at payment are the same.

- **Tournament entry fee** — a one-time payment **per team**, paid by the captain. It is set separately for each tournament and shown before payment. The per-player amount is for reference only. A captain with active VIP status pays a discounted fee.
- **VIP status** — a 1-month subscription that **renews automatically every month** until you cancel it in the VIP window. Access remains until the end of the paid period.
- **Voluntary support of Core League** — a voluntary one-time payment within the limits shown below. Nothing is provided for it, and it does not affect prize pools.

Payment is by Visa or Mastercard card through WayForPay, and by Apple Pay or Google Pay if they are shown on the payment page.

Refund terms are in the [Refund Policy](/refund/). The full terms of service are in the [Public Offer Agreement](/offer/).
