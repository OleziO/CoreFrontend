## 2. What data we collect

**At login and in your profile:**

- Discord: ID, username, display name, avatar;
- Steam: Steam ID, if you have linked your account;
- Telegram ID — if you entered it in your profile;
- country and city, in-game positions, rating, cities for LAN events;
- screenshots you upload as MMR proof.

**When you play:**

- match statistics from OpenDota and Steam (heroes, results, player performance);
- team rosters, participation in tournaments and duels, results.

**Communication:**

- messages in the site chat: in public channels, private messages and requests to the admins;
- notifications the site sends you.

**Payments:**

- payment records: service, amount, currency, date, payment reference, status;
- a masked card number (only a few digits) and the WayForPay regular-payment token for VIP auto-renewal;
- a record of your consent to the terms at payment (document version, date, service, amount).

We **do not receive or store** full card data (number, expiry date, CVV) — WayForPay processes it.

**Analytics:** if Google Analytics is enabled on the site — data on page visits, device and browser, and the internal player ID after login.

## 7. Deleting your account

- In your profile press **«Видалити акаунт»** ("Delete account") and confirm; or write to support.
- Deleting the account **stops VIP auto-renewal** — no further charges are made to your card.
- If you are a team captain, the captaincy passes to another main player; if there is none, the team is disbanded.
- We keep payment and consent records for as long as accounting law requires. [LAWYER TO VERIFY]

## 8. Cookies and local storage

The site stores in your browser (localStorage):

- an **authorisation token** — so you stay logged in;
- an **age confirmation flag** — so you are not asked every time.

If Google Analytics is enabled, it sets its own cookies. You can delete cookies and site data in your browser settings; you will then need to log in again.

## 9. Security

We use HTTPS, limit data access to admins and do not store card data. No method of transmitting data over the internet is completely secure, but we do everything reasonably possible to protect your data.
